# Pikop — Privacy Policy


*Last updated: September 15, 2026 — Version 0.1*

---

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data Pikop collects, why, how it is used and shared, and the rights available to Users under applicable Nigerian data protection law, principally the **Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 (NDPA)**.

## 2. Information We Collect

**2.1 Account and Identity Information**
- Name, phone number, email address, and password (or authentication credential).
- For Fulfillers and Merchant contact persons: government-issued identity document images, selfie/liveness data, date of birth, sex, and home address, collected and verified through Pikop's identity verification partners (currently Prembly and Dojah).
- For Riders and Drivers specifically: vehicle registration, licence, and (where applicable) insurance and roadworthiness documents.
- For Merchants: business name, category, address, CAC registration number (where provided), and NAFDAC number (where applicable to the Merchant's category).

**2.2 Location Data**
- Pickup and delivery addresses provided for orders.
- Real-time GPS location of a Fulfiller during an active mission, shared with the relevant Customer(s)/Receiver for live tracking, and stopped once the mission concludes.

**2.3 Payment and Financial Information**
- Payment card or bank transfer details are collected and processed by Pikop's payment partner, Paystack, and are not stored directly by Pikop beyond what is necessary for transaction records.
- Wallet balances, transaction history, and payout bank account details (for Fulfillers and Merchants).

**2.4 Communications Data**
- SMS sent via Pikop's messaging partner, Termii, including signup OTPs and, for guest (non-app) Payers/Receivers, payment links and live-tracking links.
- In-app support communications and dispute submissions, including any photos submitted as evidence.

**2.5 Usage Data**
- App interaction data, device information, and crash/diagnostic logs, collected to maintain and improve the Platform.

## 3. How We Use Information

- To create and manage accounts, and to verify Fulfiller and Merchant identity/business credentials before permitting them to operate on the Platform.
- To process orders, payments, and escrow release, including calculating and applying the fees described in our Terms & Conditions.
- To provide live tracking and delivery confirmation.
- To send transactional communications (OTPs, order updates, payment links, tracking links, receipts).
- To investigate disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce our Terms & Conditions.
- To comply with legal obligations, including responding to lawful requests from regulators or law enforcement.

## 4. How We Share Information

Pikop shares personal data with the following categories of third party, each acting for a defined purpose:

| Recipient | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| Paystack | Payment processing | Payment card/bank details, transaction amounts |
| Prembly / Dojah | Identity (KYC) verification | Identity documents, selfie/liveness data, name, DOB |
| Termii | SMS delivery (OTP, payment links, tracking links) | Phone number, message content |
| Google (Maps/Places) | Address autocomplete, mapping, live tracking display | Location queries, coordinates |
| Fulfillers | To complete an assigned mission | Receiver name, contact, and delivery address |
| Merchants | To fulfil an order | Customer/Receiver delivery details relevant to the order |
| Regulators / Law Enforcement | Legal compliance | As legally required |

Pikop does not sell personal data to third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

## 5. Data Retention

Pikop retains personal data for as long as an account remains active, and thereafter for a period necessary to satisfy legal, accounting, dispute-resolution, and regulatory requirements.

## 6. Your Rights

Subject to the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, Users may have the right to: access the personal data Pikop holds about them; request correction of inaccurate data; request deletion of data no longer necessary for the purposes it was collected; object to certain processing; and lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Requests can be made through our support channels.

## 7. Data Security

Pikop applies technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of the data involved, including restricting access to identity verification data, using secure connections for data in transit, and storing sensitive credentials (such as payment provider API keys) outside of client-side application code.

## 8. Children's Privacy

The Platform is not directed at, and Pikop does not knowingly collect personal data from, individuals under 18 years of age.

## 9. International Data Transfers

Some of Pikop's service providers (payment, identity verification, mapping, and messaging partners) may process data outside Nigeria. Where this occurs, Pikop takes steps intended to ensure an adequate level of protection consistent with NDPA requirements.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

Pikop may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be notified through the app or by email/SMS before taking effect.

## 11. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, or to exercise a data protection right, contact: **privacy@pikop.com.ng**.
