### 📱 **Screenshot Storyboard Breakdown**

#### **1. Home & Service Discovery**
*   **Play Store Slot**: Screenshot #1 (Primary)
*   **Headline Caption**: *"Four Services, One Super App"*
*   **Key Highlights**:
    *   Direct access to **Dispatch**, **Food**, **Groceries**, and **Shop**.

    *   Prominent **100% Escrow Protected** banner.

#### **2. Real-Time GPS Tracking & Security**
*   **Play Store Slot**: Screenshot #2
*   **Headline Caption**: *"Live GPS Tracking & OTP Handoffs"*
*   **Key Highlights**:
    *   Live map showing real-time rider position, speed, and ETA.

    *   One-tap phone call button to contact the fulfiller directly.

#### **3. Multi-Item Shopping Cart**
*   **Play Store Slot**: Screenshot #3
*   **Headline Caption**: *"Multi-Item Shopping Cart & Coupons"*
*   **Key Highlights**:
    *   Seamless quantity controls (`+` / `-`) for fresh produce and groceries.
    *   Store-specific promo coupon integration (`SUPERMART-500`).
    *   Transparent subtotal and checkout fare summary.

#### **4. 100% Escrow Protected Payments**
*   **Play Store Slot**: Screenshot #4
*   **Headline Caption**: *"Pay on Delivery with Complete Peace of Mind"*
*   **Key Highlights**:
    *   **Cash on Delivery (Escrow)** as the primary recommended option.
    *   Support for Card, Bank Transfer, USSD, and Pikop Wallet.
    *   Transparent 10% Escrow Platform Fee breakdown before mission activation.

#### **5. 24/7 AI Support Assistant**
*   **Play Store Slot**: Screenshot #5
*   **Headline Caption**: *"24/7 Instant AI Support Assistant"*
*   **Key Highlights**:
    *   **Pikop AI Agent** answering complex delivery and escrow questions instantly.
    *   Direct knowledge-base integration powered by Gemini.
    *   One-tap option to escalate to a human support agent.
